1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (WHAT IS REJIVAN IN ONE MINUTE?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ReJivan is an intelligent, privacy-first healthcare monitoring system designed to 
act like a vigilant, 24/7 personal nurse for elderly individuals living at home, 
as well as for understaffed hospital rooms ("Virtual Wards").

In India today, families face a terrifying dilemma: millions of elderly parents live 
alone or spend hours unmonitored while their children work. In government and 
semi-urban hospitals, a single night-shift nurse often watches over 30 to 50 beds. 
When an elderly person suffers a sudden fall, a silent stroke, a nocturnal drop in 
blood oxygen, or a heart crisis, hours can pass before anyone notices. 

ReJivan solves this critical "unmonitored gap" through a TWO-LAYER SAFETY SHIELD:

  Layer 1 (Vitals Shield): Continuous health metrics from smart wearables (heart rate, 
  blood oxygen SpO2, blood pressure, temperature, and blood glucose), scored using 
  clinically proven Early Warning systems (NEWS2).

  Layer 2 (Visual Shield): A privacy-first overhead camera sentinel that watches for 
  sudden falls, prolonged unresponsiveness, or unsafe bed exits. Crucially, ReJivan 
  NEVER records, stores, or transmits video. It turns people into mathematical 
  stick-figure coordinates right inside the room and erases the image in milliseconds.

If a crisis occurs, ReJivan executes an AUTONOMOUS 3-TIER EMERGENCY ESCALATION LADDER:
  1. Calls and alerts the primary family caregiver.
  2. If unanswered, escalates to a backup relative or neighbor.
  3. If still unanswered, automatically alerts emergency dispatch (108 / 112 / Island 
     Marine Ambulance).

2. THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING: THE "SILENT GAP" IN HEALTHCARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Medical emergencies rarely announce themselves with screams. Most fatal incidents 
in elderly care happen in complete silence:

1. The "Long Lie" After a Fall:
   Over 30% of adults over 65 fall each year. The greatest danger is not the impact 
   itself, but the "long lie"—lying on the cold floor unable to reach a phone for 
   6 to 12 hours. This causes hypothermia, muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis), 
   kidney failure, and trauma.

2. Silent Nocturnal Hypoxia (Oxygen Drops):
   Patients with respiratory illnesses or heart failure often experience severe 
   blood oxygen dips during deep sleep. Without continuous monitoring, they slip 
   into carbon dioxide narcosis or cardiac arrest without waking up.

3. The Hospital Nurse Ratio Crisis:
   In developing nations and remote regions, nurse-to-patient ratios routinely 
   exceed 1:30 at night. Nurses cannot be physically present at every bedside. A 
   post-operative patient who deteriorates between 4-hour vital checks may suffer 
   irreversible damage.

4. Geographical Isolation (The Andaman & Nicobar Reality):
   In remote archipelagoes like Andaman & Nicobar (Samrat's home), tertiary hospitals 
   (like GB Pant Hospital in Port Blair) are hours or sea-ferry journeys away from outer 
   islands like Little Andaman (Hut Bay) or Diglipur. Immediate detection is the 
   literal difference between life and death.

3. HOW REJIVAN WORKS: THE THREE CORE PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PILLAR 1: CONTINUOUS CLINICAL WEARABLES & DETERIORATION ENGINE
- Connects to everyday smart health sensors: smartwatch, pulse oximeter, blood 
  pressure cuff, continuous glucose monitor (CGM), and digital thermometer.
- Runs the National Early Warning Score 2 (NEWS2)—the gold standard scoring used by 
  the UK Royal College of Physicians and India's Ministry of Health.
- Rather than panicking over a single noisy reading, ReJivan analyzes trends:
  * Normal Baseline (Green): Stable physiological state.
  * Caution / Moderate Risk (Yellow): Stepwise vital changes requiring check-in.
  * High Risk / Danger (Red): Critical threshold breaches (e.g., HR > 130 bpm, 
    SpO2 < 88%, SBP > 180 mmHg) triggering immediate emergency response.

PILLAR 2: PRIVACY-FIRST OPTICAL SENTINEL (CAMERA ZONES)
- Standard security cameras record invasive video that elderly parents reject and 
  that violates India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
- ReJivan's solution: On-Device Computer Vision (Ultralytics YOLO-Pose / MediaPipe).
- How it works:
  1. The camera feed is ingested only in the device's volatile memory (RAM).
  2. The algorithm extracts 17 body joint keypoints (shoulders, hips, knees).
  3. The raw video frame is INSTANTLY deleted. No video is ever saved to disk or 
     sent to the cloud.
  4. Kinematic math computes downward fall velocity, torso angle, and post-event 
     stillness.
  5. If the person falls and does not get up, or if an elderly dementia patient leaves 
     the bed and vanishes from the room at 3 AM, an alert is raised immediately.

PILLAR 3: AUTONOMOUS 3-TIER EMERGENCY ESCALATION LADDER
- When danger is verified, ReJivan does not just sound a local beep. It triggers 
  an escalating rescue sequence:
  * Stage 1 (0 to 15 seconds): Push notification, loud siren, and automated phone 
    call to the primary family member (e.g., Anita's daughter Priya).
  * Stage 2 (15 to 45 seconds): If unacknowledged, calls the backup caregiver or 
    designated neighbor (e.g., Dr. Rajesh).
  * Stage 3 (45+ seconds): Dispatches emergency medical services (108 Ambulance / 
    112 National Emergency / Port Blair Marine Ambulance) transmitting the patient's 
    exact location, baseline medical history, and vital crisis data.

4. DUAL DEPLOYMENT: HOME CARE VS. HOSPITAL VIRTUAL WARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ReJivan was intentionally engineered with dual operational modes:

MODE A: FAMILY HOME DASHBOARD
- Designed for sons, daughters, and family caregivers.
- Warm, easy-to-read interface available in 5 Indian languages (English, Hindi, 
  Bengali, Tamil, Telugu).
- Features:
  * Live vital cards with color-coded safety indicators.
  * Medication Administration Record (MAR): shows daily medicine schedules, 
    dosages, and missed-dose warnings.
  * Privacy Radar: shows real-time posture and movement indicators without video.
  * One-tap direct caregiver voice check-in.

MODE B: HOSPITAL "VIRTUAL WARD" (NURSE STATION VIEW)
- Accessible only to healthcare staff and ward nurses.
- Central Multi-Bed Telemetry Grid:
  * Simultaneously monitors multiple beds (e.g., Bed 101 Anita Sharma, Bed 102 
    Ram Prakash, Bed 103 Meera Nair, Bed 104 Kavitha Rao).
  * Beds automatically sort by NEWS2 acuity score; deteriorating beds flash at the 
    top of the nurse's screen.
- 4-Stage Clinician Alert Lifecycle:
  * Stage 1: [New] — Alert generated by threshold or camera incident.
  * Stage 2: [Acknowledged] — Nurse accepts the alert, logging nurse ID and timestamp.
  * Stage 3: [Follow-up Recorded] — Nurse documents clinical actions (e.g., "Oxygen 
    mask administered at 4L/min, IV cannula flushed").
  * Stage 4: [Resolved] — Patient stabilized, closing the audit loop with persistent 
    medical records compliant with ABDM / Ayushman Bharat standards.

5. TAILORED FOR ANDAMAN & NICOBAR ISLANDS & REMOTE REGIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ReJivan is not an ivory-tower Silicon Valley demo that crashes when Wi-Fi drops. 
It is specifically hardened for remote, resource-constrained environments:

1. 100% Offline Native Android App:
   Located in `app-android/`, this Kotlin + Jetpack Compose application contains 
   the entire vital rules engine, emergency escalation timers, and local storage 
   embedded right inside the phone. It operates flawlessly with ZERO internet 
   connection, making it ideal for outer islands like Little Andaman or Nicobar.

2. On-Device Edge Compute:
   Vision processing runs on local mini-PCs or laptops equipped with standard 
   graphics hardware (NVIDIA RTX/GTX or lightweight CPU pose estimation). Video 
   never strains island internet bandwidth.

3. Low-Bandwidth SMS / Cellular Fallback:
   When high-speed data networks fail, emergency alarms revert to plain 2G cellular 
   SMS and automated telephone interactive voice response (IVR).

6. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE (WHAT IS INSIDE THIS PROJECT?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ReJivan project folder contains a complete, production-grade ecosystem:

1. Web Application & Backend (`prototype/`):
   - Built on Node.js, Express, and React.
   - Deployed live on Vercel at `https://rejivan.vercel.app` and locally on port 8080.
   - Stateless HMAC-SHA256 authentication with role-based access control (Caregiver vs. 
     Hospital Nurse).
   - Authoritative simulation and telemetry store (`prototype/simulation-service.js`).

2. Administrator & Judge Dual-Interface System (`/admin/simulator`):
   - To make hackathon demonstrations seamless, we created a protected admin cockpit.
   - The Judge-facing dashboard is 100% clean, professional, and free of sliders or 
     "fake" demo buttons.
   - A teammate in a separate window or mobile phone logs into `/admin/simulator` 
     (private team developer key required) and can trigger realistic clinical scenarios:
     * Normal Baseline
     * Elevated Heart Rate (Tachycardia: 138 bpm)
     * Low Blood Oxygen (Hypoxemia: 88%)
     * Blood Pressure Warning (Hypertensive Crisis: 178/104 mmHg)
     * Multiple Abnormalities (Septic / Shock State)
     * 15-second Stepwise Gradual Recovery
     * Network Interruption / Offline Simulation
   - When triggered in the Admin window, the shared backend updates and the Judge's 
     dashboard updates live within 1.8 seconds without refreshing!

3. Real-Time Vision Daemon (`tools/yolo_edge_sentinel.py`):
   - Runs Ultralytics YOLO-Pose keypoint inference on hardware GPU (RTX 4060 / GTX 1650).
   - Streams live optical kinematics, torso tilt angles, velocity, and patient presence 
     over a local MJPEG reverse-proxy (`/api/yolo/*`).
   - Includes automatic patient disappearance alerting (alerts if an unsteady patient 
     exits the bed and leaves the room unattended).

4. Native Android Application (`app-android/`):
   - Standalone native Kotlin application mirroring all web features offline.
   - Packaged debug APK (`ReJivan_v1.0.apk`) ready for deployment on Android devices.

WHAT IS FULLY REAL IN THE CODEBASE TODAY:

  [REAL] The on-device YOLO-Pose computer vision pipeline, skeletal keypoint extraction,

  [REAL] The native Android application running embedded offline logic.
  [REAL] 5-language localization engine (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, English).
  [REAL] Real-time dual-window state synchronization between Admin and Judge views.

WHAT IS SIMULATED FOR SAFE DEMONSTRATION:
  [SIMULATED] Physical Wearable Bluetooth Hardware Feeds:
    Instead of strapping physical pulse oximeters and BP cuffs to the judges' wrists, 
    realistic vital telemetry is generated and controlled via the Admin Simulator.
  [SIMULATED] Telecom Carrier Emergency Dispatch:
    The full escalation queue, timer ladder, retry state machine, and siren triggers 
    are 100% real code; however, the final telephony connection to 108/112 emergency 
    dispatchers is simulated in software so that testing does not place false emergency 
    calls to government helplines.

8. HOW TO RUN AND DEMONSTRATE REJIVAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Start the System on Any Windows Computer:
   - Double-click `tools\run_yolo.bat` to launch the local AI Vision sentinel.
   - Open a terminal in the folder and run `npm start` (or `node prototype/server.js`).
   - The web app is now running at: http://localhost:8080

2. Demonstrate Live to Judges:
   - Window 1 (Judge View): Open `http://localhost:8080` (or `https://rejivan.vercel.app`).
     * Log in as Family Caregiver: `asharma@demo.in` / `demo123`
     * Or log in as Hospital Nurse: `wardnurse@demo.in` / `demo123`
     * Note the pristine, professional hospital dashboard.

       a clinical alert with the exact medical threshold reason.

       and resolve the alert!

9. SUMMARY FOR EVALUATORS & THE FUTURE ROADMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ReJivan proves that proactive, life-saving healthcare monitoring does not require 
invasive surveillance or multi-lakh hospital hardware. By combining affordable 
wearables, privacy-respecting edge computer vision, and an autonomous emergency ladder, 
ReJivan bridges the fatal gap between doctor visits and nurse rounds.

Roadmap:
- Phase 1 (Completed): Working prototype, dual-interface demo, YOLO sentinel, Android app.
- Phase 2 (HSC 2027 Submission): Pilot testing with community health workers (ASHAs) 
  in Port Blair and integration with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) health IDs.
- Phase 3 (Commercialization): Low-cost "Made in India" home gateway hardware (<₹5,000) 
  subsidized through public healthcare programs.
