Problem Definition: The "Silent Gap" in Care
Vulnerabilities in Solo Elderly Care, Hospital Ward Overburden, and Island Geography
Slide 2 / 7

1. The Post-Fall "Long Lie"

30% of seniors fall annually. When an elder falls and cannot reach a phone for 6–12 hours, rapid rhabdomyolysis (muscle breakdown), pressure ulcers, hypothermia, and kidney failure set in.

Fatal Statistic: Over 50% of elders who experience a "long lie" do not survive past 6 months.

2. Silent Nocturnal Hypoxia

COPD, heart failure, and post-viral pulmonary patients suffer severe oxygen desaturation below 85% during deep sleep without waking up.

Hospital Crises: Night nurse-to-patient ratios exceed 1:30 in government wards, leaving hemodynamic deterioration unspotted between 4-hour manual rounds.

3. Andaman & Nicobar Island Reality

36 inhabited islands with only one tertiary referral center (GB Pant Hospital, Port Blair). Outer islands (Hut Bay, Little Andaman, Car Nicobar) face 6–10 hour sea transit delays.

The Need: An offline-first, autonomous monitoring sentinel that detects deterioration immediately, without relying on stable internet or active panic buttons.

Why Existing Products Fail: Traditional panic buttons require the victim to be conscious to press them. Commercial smartwatches (Apple/Galaxy) suffer from 80%+ false alarms during hand gestures, cost ₹30,000–₹80,000, and cannot be afforded by rural families.

Proposed Solution: The Arohasetu Architecture
Two-Layer Safety Shield + Autonomous 3-Tier Escalation Ladder + Dual Deployment
Slide 3 / 7

Layer 1: Wearable Physiological Shield

  • Ingests vital biometrics: Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Pressure, Temperature, Respiratory Rate, Blood Glucose.
  • Analyzes trends via the clinically proven NEWS2 (National Early Warning Score 2) deterioration matrix.
  • Replaces black-box generative AI with auditable, deterministic clinical rules to eliminate medical hallucinations.

Layer 2: Privacy-First Optical Sentinel

  • Uses an overhead commodity webcam running on-device edge AI (Ultralytics YOLO-Pose).
  • Extracts 17 skeletal joint coordinates at 25+ FPS to detect sudden falls, floor stillness, and wandering.
  • 100% Video-Private: Zero video is recorded, stored, or streamed to the cloud (DPDP Act 2023 compliant).

Autonomous 3-Tier Fail-Safe Escalation Ladder

Tier 1 (0–15s): Instant push notification and automated voice call to primary family caregiver.
Tier 2 (15–45s): Automated escalation to secondary contact, neighbor, or attending physician if unacknowledged.
Tier 3 (45s+): Autonomous emergency dispatch (108 / 112 / Island Marine Ambulance) with telemetry payload.

  • 5. Alarm Rate-Limiting: Groups repetitive alerts to eliminate alarm fatigue.

  • 7. Multi-Modal Graceful Degradation: Vision watch persists if wearables die.
