1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

ReJivan is an end-to-end, multi-tenant digital nursing and remote health surveillance platform. It bridges the critical healthcare accessibility and monitoring gap for elderly residents living independently and inpatients in understaffed public hospital wards. The platform unites two independent, complementary sensory pillars:

  • Continuous Bio-Telemetry & Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM): Real-time tracking of five vital signs (Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Pressure, Temperature, Blood Glucose) evaluated against the UK Royal College of Physicians and Indian MoHFW NEWS2 clinical deterioration standards.
  • Privacy-First Optical & Kinematic Vision Sentinel: Real-time edge computer vision tracking body movement and skeletal pose to detect dangerous bed-falls, slips, collapses, and prolonged immobility—without recording, transmitting, or storing raw video (DPDP Act 2023 compliant).
Geographic Anchoring: Andaman & Nicobar Healthcare Reality
The archipelago comprises 36 inhabited islands with only one major tertiary referral hospital (GB Pant Hospital, Port Blair). Outer-island facilities (e.g. Hut Bay on Little Andaman, Car Nicobar) suffer severe specialist shortages. Sea-ambulance or ferry transfers require 6 to 14 hours. ReJivan provides continuous home monitoring for island seniors and empowers regional hospital wards with centralized "Virtual Ward" telemetry, detecting emergencies early enough to enable timely intervention.

2. Dual Deployment Paradigm (Home Care vs. Hospital Virtual Ward)

ReJivan operates identically and securely across two distinct healthcare environments with strict role-based data isolation:

Environment A: Home Care Mode

Intended Users: Family members and private caregivers monitoring elderly relatives at home.

  • Demo Personas: Anita Sharma (Port Blair, hypertension) and Ram Prakash (Little Andaman, diabetes).
  • Data Isolation: Caregivers only see their own family member's vitals, MAR schedule, and room camera.
  • Role Protection: Hospital Virtual Ward is completely hidden and guarded against family access.
  • Autonomous Help: Initiates automated calls to primary family, backup relative, and 108 ambulance.

Environment B: Hospital "Virtual Ward" Mode

Intended Users: Hospital ward nurses, duty medical officers, and centralized nursing stations.

  • Demo Persona: GB Pant Hospital Ward Nurse (Shift A Lead).
  • Multi-Bed Console: Simultaneous monitoring of Beds 101, 102, 103, and 104 in Ward A.
  • Bed Selector Bar: Instant toggle between "All Beds (Ward Grid)" and individual bed telemetry deep-dives.
  • Clinical Escalation: Routes critical deterioration immediately to the hospital Medical Emergency Team (MET) and Code Blue crash team.

6. Physiological Telemetry, Bio-Drift & Clinical Rules Engine

ReJivan provides continuous, realistic physiological simulation that mimics actual human hemodynamics while evaluating clinical safety in real time.

6.1 Continuous 1.5-Second Bio-Drift Engine

Rather than presenting static values, the platform implements a mathematical physiological drift engine:

  • Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia (RSA): Heart rate drifts sinusoidally by ±2–4 bpm synchronized with normal breathing phases.
  • Arterial Baroreflex Jitter: Blood pressure exhibits natural coupled variations (systolic ±3–6 mmHg, diastolic ±2–4 mmHg).
  • Micro-Oxygenation Variations: Capillary SpO2 micro-fluctuations (±0.2–0.5%) reflecting peripheral perfusion dynamics.
  • FIFO Rolling Buffers: All sparklines (HR, SpO2, BP, Temp, Glucose) update on each 1.5s pulse beat, visibly animating in real time.

6.2 Evaluator Interactive Scenario Injection

Evaluators and competition judges can test the system's clinical reactivity in real time using dedicated triage injection buttons:

🟢 Normal Baseline: Stabilizes vitals to safe physiological baselines (HR 78, SpO2 98%, BP 122/80, Temp 36.9°C, Glucose 105).
⚠️ BP Crisis (Hypertensive Urgency): Drives BP to 172/106 mmHg; elevates NEWS2 score to Caution; alerts attending physician.
🚨 Acute Hypoxemia: Drops SpO2 to 89% (desaturation below 92% threshold); triggers immediate high-priority alarm.
📉 Severe Bradycardia: Drops heart rate to 50 bpm; triggers hemodynamic instability watch and care team notifications.

8. Privacy-First Edge Vision Sentinel & Kinematic Motion Engine

ReJivan's second sensory pillar provides physical safety monitoring for elderly individuals and hospital bed-fall prevention using on-device computer vision.

8.1 Dual-Vision Engine Architecture

Hardware Ultralytics YOLO-Pose (Port 5050)

  • Captures local webcam or CCTV at ~25–58 FPS.
  • Hardware acceleration via NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 (CUDA).
  • Extracts 17 COCO skeletal landmarks in real time.
  • Calculates Center-of-Mass downward velocity (dy/dt) and torso inclination angle (0° upright to 90° horizontal).
  • Exposes telemetry via /api/yolo/telemetry and frame processing via POST /api/yolo/process_frame.

In-Browser Client-Side Fallback Engine

  • Runs 100% inside any modern web browser on rejivan2.vercel.app without local Python installation.
  • Uses real-time optical pixel differencing on an offscreen buffer (64×48) to track physical body energy % and dynamic motion centroids.
  • Seamlessly connects to local YOLO daemon when reachable, overlaying green skeletal wireframes directly on the video canvas.
  • Smooth 1.8s keypoint decay and watchdog protection prevent stream interruption.

8.2 DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & On-Demand Hardware Control

Zero Video Recorded or Stored: Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, continuous camera surveillance of private residences and patient rooms presents severe privacy hazards. ReJivan enforces strict privacy-by-design:
1. Physical Camera LED OFF by Default: Physical camera hardware is never opened at daemon boot. The camera only opens on-demand when an authorized user initiates monitoring, and is released (cap.release()) the instant the user pauses or navigates away.
2. DPDP Privacy Radar Mode: Blanks out raw video frames completely with a dark clinical radar grid, displaying only skeletal joint lines and velocity vectors.
3. Instant Frame Deletion: Video frames exist in volatile memory for under 20 milliseconds during inference and are immediately discarded. Zero video or photographic images are ever written to disk or uploaded to any server.

9. Autonomous 3-Tier Emergency Escalation Ladder

When a critical vital threshold or confirmed physical fall is identified, ReJivan executes a deterministic, multi-channel escalation ladder without human delay:

Step 0: Resident Safety Check-In Dialog (30-Second Grace Window)

An interactive emergency modal appears on screen accompanied by an audible warning chime: "Did you fall or drop your device?". The resident can tap "I'm Okay" or "Device Drop" to cancel the alert immediately. Furthermore, if the vision sentinel detects that the resident has stood back up into an upright posture within 5 seconds, the emergency sequence automatically cancels.

Tier 1: Primary Family Caregiver

Immediate automated call and WhatsApp/SMS alert to primary contact (Priya Sharma in Port Blair / Rajesh Prakash in Little Andaman). 4.2s answer attempt window.

Tier 2: Backup Relative / Neighbor

If Tier 1 is unanswered, cascades automatically to the secondary family contact or trusted island neighbor within 5.5s to 9.8s.

Tier 3: 108 / 112 Ambulance Dispatch

If unconfirmed, dispatches island ambulance services automatically with GPS coordinates, baseline conditions, and real-time vitals telemetry.

10. Seven Built-In Clinical Reliability Safeguards

Safeguard Mechanism Technical Implementation Clinical Protection Benefit
1. Physiologic Validation Rejects physiologically impossible values (HR > 250 or < 30, SpO2 < 50%, Systolic BP > 260). Prevents sensor detachment noise from corrupting clinical telemetry.
2. Confidence Scoring Dynamic 0–100% score weighting device tier (medical vs. simulated) and edge signal stability. Allows clinicians to immediately gauge data trustworthiness.
3. Consecutive Verification Danger states must persist across 2+ consecutive measurement epochs before dispatching Tier 3. Prevents transient hardware glitches from calling ambulances.
4. Sensor Heartbeat Watchdog Monitors last-seen timestamps; flags SENSOR_OFFLINE if telemetry ceases for > 2 minutes. Warns family if a wearable has fallen off, died, or lost Bluetooth range.
5. Alert Rate-Limiting Caps non-escalating alerts to a maximum of 5 alerts per patient per 5 minutes. Eliminates alarm fatigue for attending nurses and caregivers.
6. Immutable Audit Trail Every measurement, threshold breach, nurse check-off, and call is permanently logged with timestamps. Provides full legal and clinical accountability for hospital compliance.
7. Graceful Degradation System functions seamlessly with partial sensors (e.g. vision offline, vitals active). Ensures continuous safety monitoring even during hardware maintenance.
