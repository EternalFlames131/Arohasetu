1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

Arohasetu is an indigenous, frugal, privacy-first remote patient monitoring and autonomous emergency response ecosystem. Designed to solve India’s acute rural and elderly healthcare crises, Arohasetu functions as a tireless, 24/7 personal nurse operating across two vulnerable environments: Home Care (for elders living alone while family works) and Hospital "Virtual Wards" (where night nurse-to-patient ratios exceed 1:30).

Instead of relying on passive wristwatches that fail when an elder falls unconscious, Arohasetu combines a Two-Layer Safety Shield (wearable multi-vital physiological monitoring + privacy-first on-device computer vision) with an Autonomous 3-Tier Escalation Ladder that directly notifies family and dispatches government emergency services (108/112/marine ambulance) without requiring user intervention.

Layer 1: Wearable Physiological Shield

Ingests clinical vitals (Heart Rate, SpO2, Blood Pressure, Temperature, Respiratory Rate, Blood Glucose) from BLE medical sensors. Telemetry is evaluated against the clinically validated NEWS2 (National Early Warning Score 2) deterioration matrix to catch impending crashes hours before collapse.

Layer 2: Privacy-First Optical Sentinel

Uses a standard overhead room webcam running local edge AI (Ultralytics YOLO-Pose 17-keypoint skeletal tracking). Detects sudden falls, bed-exit wandering, and floor immobility. 100% video-private: no video is ever recorded, stored, or streamed to the cloud (DPDP Act 2023 compliant).

2. The Problem Matrix: 9 Clinical & Social Crises Arohasetu Solves

Medical disasters among senior citizens and post-operative patients occur in silence. Arohasetu provides real-time intervention for nine life-threatening scenarios:

Crisis / Medical Hazard The Existing Systemic Failure Arohasetu's Autonomous Intervention
1. Post-Fall "Long Lie" & Rhabdomyolysis Over 30% of elders fall annually. Lying helpless on the floor for 6–12 hours causes hypothermia, muscle necrosis, kidney failure, and 50% mortality within 6 months. Optical kinematics detect high-velocity descent and prolonged ground stillness, launching emergency escalation within 30 seconds.
2. Silent Nocturnal Hypoxia Elderly patients with COPD, post-COVID fibrosis, or heart failure experience severe desaturation during sleep without waking up, leading to silent cardiac arrest. Continuous SpO2 tracking alerts caregivers when oxygen saturation dips below 90%, triggering audible alarms to wake the patient.
3. Diabetic Coma & Hypoglycemia Sudden drops in blood glucose cause rapid cognitive collapse and unconsciousness before the elder can test their blood or ingest sugar. Integrates continuous glucose telemetry (FreeStyle Libre / CGM profile) with rate-of-fall velocity alerts before hypoglycemic shock occurs.
4. Medication Non-Adherence & Polypharmacy Elderly patients take 5–8 daily tablets; forgetting blood pressure drugs causes hypertensive stroke, while accidental double-dosing induces fatal hypotension. Account-tailored digital MAR (Medication Administration Record) with bilingual push reminders and automated alerts to children if doses are missed.
5. Bed-Exit Wandering & Dementia Delirium Demented or delirious post-surgical patients wander out of bed at 3 AM, falling in dark hallways or unmonitored hospital bathrooms. Presence state machine detects patient exiting bed/camera zone, raising a "Patient Left Bed Unattended" caution alert before a fall happens.
6. Hospital Ward Understaffing (1:30 Ratio) Ward nurses can only manually check vitals every 4–6 hours. Post-op complications between nursing rounds go undetected until cardiac arrest. Virtual Ward central console monitors all beds simultaneously, auto-ranking patients by clinical acuity so nurses intercept deterioration early.
7. Sensor False-Alarm Fatigue Consumer watches generate 80%+ false fall alerts when dropped or tapped, causing families to disable notifications. Bi-modal sensor fusion: verifies kinematic impact, posture angle, lack of biometric recovery, and a 15–30s pre-alert verification window.
8. Island & Rural Healthcare Isolation In Andaman & Nicobar (36 inhabited islands), tertiary care is concentrated at GB Pant Hospital, Port Blair; outer islands are 6–10 hours away by sea. Offline-first local intelligence enables local primary health centers and families to stabilize patients and summon marine ambulances immediately.
9. Family Caregiver Burnout & Guilt Working children experience severe anxiety when unable to reach elderly parents, leading to chronic stress, loss of work focus, and burnout. Live remote Family Monitor with 5 Indian languages, WhatsApp/SMS fallback, and the Prajñā AI Care Assistant answering natural-language health queries.

3. Comprehensive Technical Architecture

Arohasetu is built on a resilient three-tier software and edge-hardware architecture engineered to operate reliably under severe bandwidth constraints, submarine cable cuts, and hardware disconnects:

Tier 1: Ingestion & Edge Vision

Medical Hardware: Pairs with BLE vitals monitors (ECG, Pulse Oximeter, BP Cuff, Continuous Glucose, Temp patch).
Edge YOLO Sentinel: Python daemon (tools/yolo_edge_sentinel.py) running Ultralytics YOLO-Pose on local GPU/CPU. Extracts 17 COCO skeletal keypoints at 25+ FPS. Zero raw image storage.

Tier 2: Clinical Deterioration Engine

Deterministic NEWS2 Core: Standardized Royal College of Physicians scoring. Eliminates generative LLM hallucinations in clinical triage.
State Machine: Tracks 7-stage kinematic fall progression (Upright → Rapid Descent → Impact → Inversion → Stillness → Verification → Confirmed Emergency).

Tier 3: Autonomous Escalation

Fail-Safe Ladder: Escalates automatically across 3 tiers (Family → Neighbor → 108/112 EMS dispatch).
Dual Deployment: Family Caregiver Dashboard (5 Indian languages) + Hospital Virtual Ward (multi-bed matrix, 4-stage nurse lifecycle, and ABDM compliance).

4. The Autonomous 3-Tier Escalation Ladder

Tier 1 (0–15s): Primary Family Caregiver Push + Call

Instant push notification and automated phone call to the primary caregiver (e.g., daughter Priya in Port Blair). Live vitals telemetry and room location are transmitted immediately.

Tier 2 (15–45s): Secondary / Neighbor Auto-Escalation

If Tier 1 fails to acknowledge within 30 seconds, Arohasetu immediately calls the secondary contact (trusted local neighbor, building warden, or attending family physician).

Tier 3 (45s+): Government EMS (108/112) Emergency Dispatch

If no human acknowledges, the system autonomously dispatches government emergency response (108 Ambulance / Port Blair Marine Ambulance) transmitting GPS, clinical telemetry, and medical history.

5. Dual Deployment Modes: Home Care vs. Hospital Virtual Ward

Mode A: Home Caregiver Portal

Elderly & Family
  • 5 Indian Languages: Complete localization in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Bengali (বাংলা), Tamil (தமிழ்), and Telugu (తెలుగు).
  • Prajñā AI Care Assistant: Natural-language health conversational agent grounded in the patient's real-time vitals and medication history.
  • Smart Medication Tracker: Daily dosage schedule, adherence tracking, and missed-dose push notifications.
  • Privacy-First Posture Radar: Displays real-time kinematic status (Standing, Seated, Supine) without exposing video feeds.

Mode B: Hospital "Virtual Ward"

Clinical & Ward Nurse
  • Multi-Bed Central Telemetry: Live monitoring grid tracking Bed 101 to Bed 104 with dynamic clinical acuity sorting.
  • Standard NEWS2 Acuity Scoring: Categorizes patients into Low (0–4), Medium (5–6), and High Risk (≥7) according to MoHFW standards.
  • 4-Stage Clinician Lifecycle: Structured alert resolution protocol: New → Acknowledged → Follow-up Recorded → Resolved.
  • ABDM-Compliant Audit Trails: Timestamped clinical follow-up notes exportable to standard Electronic Health Records.

6. Seven Built-In Clinical Reliability Safeguards

1. Physiological Range Validation: Rejects sensor artifact noise (e.g., HR > 240 or SpO2 < 50% without pulse waveform) to prevent false alerts.
2. Device Confidence Indexing: Continuously rates sensor signal quality (0–100%) and alerts nurses when sensors slip or lose skin contact.
3. Consecutive Verification Time-Window: Sustained danger readings across consecutive sampling beats required before triggering Tier 3 escalation.
4. Sensor Heartbeat Watchdog: Disconnecting or powering off a sensor immediately triggers an offline warning on dashboard and simulator.
5. Intelligent Alarm Rate-Limiting: Groups identical biometric warnings within sliding 5-minute windows, completely eliminating clinician alarm fatigue.
6. Multi-Modal Graceful Degradation: If wearables run out of battery, the optical fall watch stays active; if camera is occluded, vitals watch persists.

11. Evaluator Quick-Start Demonstration Guide (Dual-Window Live Sync)

Judges can verify Arohasetu’s live clinical reactivity in less than 3 minutes using two browser windows (or a laptop and mobile phone):

  1. Step 1: Open Judge Clinical Dashboard
    Navigate to https://arohasetu.vercel.app (or http://localhost:8080). Log in as Hospital Nurse (wardnurse@demo.in / demo123). Point out the clean, uncluttered Virtual Ward telemetry grid displaying Beds 101 to 104 with live physiological drift and NEWS2 scores.
  2. Step 2: Open Admin Simulator Cockpit
    In a second browser tab or mobile device, open https://arohasetu.vercel.app/admin/simulator and enter the developer passcode.
  3. Step 3: Trigger a Physiological Emergency Preset
    Click "Elevated HR (138 bpm)" or "Low SpO2 (88%)", or toggle a device disconnect (e.g., Omron BP Cuff).
  4. Step 4: Witness Sub-2-Second Live Synchronization
    Switch back to the Judge tab without refreshing the page. Within 1.8 seconds, Bed 101 dynamically shifts to Amber/Red. A critical alert is prepended displaying the exact clinical trigger: "Heart Rate critical elevation: 138 bpm (threshold: >100 bpm)".
  5. Step 5: Demonstrate Nurse Resolution Workflow
    Click "Acknowledge", enter clinician notes under "Document Follow-up" (e.g., "Administered 5L O2 via nasal cannula"), and click "Resolve" to complete the auditable clinical record.
