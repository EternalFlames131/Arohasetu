PROBLEM 01

Unobserved Fall or Decline

Older adults living alone risk acute injury or sudden deterioration without immediate help.

IMPLEMENTED

Camera Pose Analysis: OpenCV + Ultralytics YOLO-Pose detects fall and immobility states locally without recording video.

PROBLEM 02

Remote Family Uncertainty

Families living far away lack real-time visibility into an elder's immediate condition.

IMPLEMENTED

Caregiver Notification: Dedicated web dashboard with configured alerts, medication MAR, and live status feeds.

PROBLEM 03

Ward Monitoring Bottlenecks

Clinical teams struggle to watch multiple dispersed patients across island wards continuously.

IMPLEMENTED

Virtual-Ward Triage: Nurse workflow engine running deterministic NEWS2 clinical risk scores.

PROBLEM 04

Geographic Care Latency

Island and rural communities encounter transport and communication delays reaching care.

SIMULATED / PLANNED

Dual-Mode Triage: Home mode paired with hardware sensor telemetry (Planned) and offline app (Implemented).

Core Innovation: Combines physiological telemetry with privacy-conscious on-device pose estimation and deterministic clinical scoring under home and virtual-ward modes. Fully transparent: no claim of unassisted medical diagnosis.

FEASIBILITY AND RISK ANALYSIS
Prototyped Feasibility & Six Managed Risk Factors
HSC|AN|00004
Team: ArohaSetu

01 Clinical Accuracy

Risk: Algorithmic misinterpretation of physiological instability.

Mitigation: Use standardized NEWS2 clinical guidance; mandate clinician-in-the-loop oversight.

02 Island Network Connectivity

Risk: Intermittent remote Internet connection in island geography.

Mitigation: Local edge alert caching on Kotlin Android client; explicit indicators when cloud sync is offline.

03 False Fall Alarms

Risk: Alert fatigue caused by sitting, bending, or dropping devices.

Mitigation: Multi-frame temporal windows (>3s floor immobility); 23/23 false-positive scenarios verified with 100% precision.

04 Escalation Integrity

Risk: External ambulance dispatch services receiving false alerts.

Mitigation: Explicit simulation labeling; multi-tier ladder (Family → Backup → 108/112) with verification step.

05 Patient Privacy

Risk: Reluctance to place optical sensors in domestic private spaces.

Mitigation: Strictly on-device keypoint inference; no raw video retention or transmission; DPDP Act compliant.

06 Clinical & Regulatory Readiness

Risk: Premature deployment without biomedical certification.

Mitigation: Positioned as assistive decision-support; dedicated clinical governance before formal trials.

Feasibility Status: Web simulator, local Python pose detector, and offline-capable Android APK functional for demonstration; live physical telemetry requires field development.

Challenges, Motivation & Journey

Why We Built ArohaSetu:
Living across the Andaman & Nicobar archipelago, medical facilities are separated by deep water and limited ferry routes. When an elder falls or deteriorates alone, delayed awareness is fatal. We built ArohaSetu to provide a watchful, personal digital nurse for every family and overburdened hospital bed.

Key Engineering Hurdle:
Achieving dependable fall detection on low-cost hardware without streaming video to external servers. We engineered a local YOLO-Pose kinematic sentinel with temporal posture tracking (>50° tilt, downward velocity drop, >3s immobility) that achieved 100% precision across 23 false-positive test scenarios.
